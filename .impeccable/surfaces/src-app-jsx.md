# Portal Teknik Komputer dan Jaringan

## Scope and visitor mode
Single-page responsive profile and learning portal for Teknik Komputer dan Jaringan at SMKN 1 Garut. Operate is primary; profile and contact content also supports reading.

## Audience and task
Current TKJ students find learning material and try the quiz and attendance flows. Prospective students and parents understand the TKJ program and find the school's official contact and address.

## Action and content
Navigate among the TKJ profile, honest LMS empty state, five-question quiz sample, browser-only attendance simulation, combined vision/mission, and official contact details.

## Constraints
The quiz is illustrative and not an official assessment. Attendance is a non-persistent simulation; do not enter real student information. Do not invent published course modules, school policy, coordinates, or official map URLs.

## Chosen direction
Peta Topologi Pembelajaran: crisp navy topology lines and labeled nodes form both the TKJ hero diagram and the page's navigable structure. White surfaces carry learning tasks; electric blue and cyan mark connection and active controls.

## Memorable moment
The hero's network topology links the program's actual areas—computers, networks, and servers—to the learning activities below, without claiming a live network or official course sequence.

## Unresolved decisions
Published LMS content and any production backend, account/attendance policy, or official assessment workflow have not been supplied.
